CHICAGO ParaArts
A new Chicago arts organization

What if the stage had room for everyone?

Chicago ParaArts creates and presents high-caliber orchestral, chamber, and theatrical work made by disabled and non-disabled artists performing as equals through ParaOrchestra and ParaTheatre, and trains the next generation of disabled musicians through accessible music education.

Why now

Chicago has the disability-services infrastructure. It doesn't yet have the stage.

We're currently filing for 501(c)(3) status and building our founding board. Chicago ParaArts exists to close that gap, one orchestra, one theatre company, and one school at a time. BandWith Chicago is already backing that work, donating rehearsal space at The Center while we get there.

What we do

Three programs, one mission.

Orchestral

ParaOrchestra

Our performance ensemble: professional-level orchestral and chamber repertoire, rehearsed and staged so that disabled and non-disabled musicians are equally featured, equally rehearsed, and equally credited. Season and venue details are being finalized alongside our founding board.

Sponsor a Student & the Access Fund

Rather than an income-based sliding scale, two simpler mechanisms let families self-identify need: Sponsor a Student covers staff-taught lesson spots entirely through donors, no documentation required. The Access Fund covers the real added cost of teaching a specific student well, things like a longer slot, a support person, or adaptive equipment, funded automatically by the price gap between Ty Chiko's and staff rates, plus donor gifts.

Rate-differential revenue

Automatic. The price gap between a Ty Chiko lesson and a staff lesson ($15 to $25 depending on length) flows to the Access Fund on every booking. Not a cut of staff pay.

Donor gifts

Open-ended, with no fixed annual target. Fills whatever the rate-differential revenue doesn't cover for accessibility costs like longer slots, a support person, or adaptive equipment.
